### Local First

is a non-profit organization that leads the development of an economy grounded in local ownership that meets the basic needs of people, builds local wealth and social capital, functions in harmony with our ecosystem, and encourages joyful community.

![Local First logo: a red spiral icon followed by the text 'Local First' and the tagline 'support locally owned business'.](7024c5b6dbff1485ff5a9be7ac846f88_5_img.webp)

Local isn't a place, it's people. It's reintegrating our lives, connecting in practical ways with the people we live and work with. Local First is another way of saying "People first."

![A blue banner featuring the 'Local First' logo on the left and the 'Local First' logo on the right.](7024c5b6dbff1485ff5a9be7ac846f88_7_img.webp)

Local isn't just a place on a map. It's people, your neighbors and their families, their businesses, farms, nonprofits, events, and recreational venues. Local is a community and all of the opportunities we create together and challenges we work to overcome. Local First has a passion for people living and working together in sustainable community.

#### When we say local, we mean:

- ✓ **Locally Owned**
- ✓ **Headquartered in West Michigan**
- ✓ **Privately Held (not traded on the stock market)**

#### By eating locally, you will:

- ✓ **Support our farmers**
- ✓ **Strengthen our local economy**
- ✓ **Ensure that you receive high quality food**
- ✓ **Cultivate community**
- ✓ **Eat sustainably while increasing healthy options**

### Why Local First?

1. 1 **Local First Engages.** It creates and deepens relationships within a community, which becomes more vibrant through collaboration and mutual investment.
2. 2 **Local First Humanizes.** Physical and economic development occurs on a scale that nurtures people and encourages beauty in natural and sustainable environments.
3. 3 **Local First Preserves.** When our citizens are given opportunities, preserving local economic and environmental value is of mutual interest.
4. 4 **Local First Strengthens.** When citizens and local businesses work together, it catalyzes positive development and the community has increased integrity in natural economic strategies.
5. 5 **Local First Excites.** Sustainable local economies and ecosystems grow into markets that are greater than the sum of their parts.
6. 6 **Local First Empowers.** It builds connections between community and business. It builds education, direction, and discovery, making for a more vibrant and resilient local economy.
7. 7 **Local First Earths.** Re-using resources in communities, employees, and the local environment makes the entire community wealthier.
