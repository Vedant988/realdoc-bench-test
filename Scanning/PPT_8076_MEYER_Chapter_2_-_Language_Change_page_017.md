## Morphology (cont.)

*Agglutinative languages*—Meaning is expressed by very complex internal structure. Turkish is a very agglutinative language. The construction *çayıçtik* means 'We drank the tea' (*çay* = 'tea'; *iç* = 'drink'; and *tik* = 'we/past').
