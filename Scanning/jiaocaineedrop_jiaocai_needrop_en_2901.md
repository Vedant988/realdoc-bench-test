## 7 Complete the sentences with the correct form of the verbs in brackets.

1. If you \_\_\_\_\_ (not understand), \_\_\_\_\_ (ask) your teacher.
2. She \_\_\_\_\_ (not be) satisfied unless her son \_\_\_\_\_ (win) first place.
3. If you \_\_\_\_\_ (see) a red light, \_\_\_\_\_ (not cross) the road.
4. You \_\_\_\_\_ (not feel) relaxed unless you \_\_\_\_\_ (learn) to let go.

用括号中动词的正确形式完成句子。

她 \_\_\_\_\_ (不) 满意, 除非她儿子 \_\_\_\_\_ (赢得) 第一。

如果你 \_\_\_\_\_ (看到) 红灯, \_\_\_\_\_ (不要过) 马路。

1 don't understand, ask 2 won't be, wins 3 see, don't cross 4 won't feel, learn
