A 2D coordinate system with axes  $x_1$  and  $x_2$ . A vector  $x$  originates from the origin. A unit vector  $a$  also originates from the origin. A dashed line extends from the tip of vector  $x$  perpendicular to the line containing vector  $a$ . The projection of  $x$  onto the line of  $a$  is a vector labeled  $x^T a$ . The origin is marked with 0.

Projection of  $x$  along the direction  $a$  ( $\|a\| = 1$ ).

$$\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} (\mathbf{a}^T \mathbf{x})$$
$$\|\mathbf{a}\| = \mathbf{a}^T \mathbf{a} = 1$$
