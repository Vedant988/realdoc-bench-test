本题:  $\because DE \parallel BC$

$$\therefore \angle 1 = \angle 2 \quad \& \quad \angle A = \angle A$$

$$\therefore \triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$$

$$\therefore \frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{2}{3} \text{ 为相似比}$$

$$\therefore \frac{S_{\triangle ADE}}{S_{\triangle ABC}} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}$$

$$\text{设 } S_{\triangle ADE} = 4x, \quad S_{\triangle ABC} = 9x$$

$$\therefore S_{\text{四边形}DBCE} = 9x - 4x = 5x$$

$$\therefore \frac{S_{\triangle ADE}}{S_{\text{四边形}DBCE}} = \frac{4x}{5x} = \frac{4}{5}$$
