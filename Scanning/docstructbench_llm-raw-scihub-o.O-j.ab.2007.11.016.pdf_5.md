Figure 1 consists of three panels (A, B, and C) illustrating the detection of antibody-antigen interactions.

**Panel A:** A high-mass MALDI mass spectrum showing Intensity (%) on the y-axis (0 to 110) versus  $m/z$  ( $\times 10^3$ ) on the x-axis (0 to 400). Two major peaks are labeled:  $[bPrP]^+$  at 23.5 kDa and  $[1E5]^+$  at 150.5 kDa.

**Panel B:** A high-mass MALDI mass spectrum showing Intensity (%) on the y-axis (0 to 110) versus  $m/z$  ( $\times 10^3$ ) on the x-axis (0 to 400). Three major peaks are labeled:  $[bPrP]^+$  at 23.5 kDa,  $[1E5\cdot bPrP]^+$  at 174.0 kDa, and  $[1E5\cdot 2bPrP]^+$  at 187.5 kDa.

**Panel C:** A sensorgram showing Response (RU) on the y-axis (2800 to 3400) versus Time (s) on the x-axis (300 to 1100). The plot shows a baseline at 2800 RU. At 500 s, an arrow indicates the "Injection of bPrP", followed by a sharp increase in response to approximately 3200 RU. A horizontal arrow labeled "Dissociation" starts at 600 s and continues to 1100 s, showing a gradual decrease in response back towards the baseline.

In the reference experiment (Fig. 4A), if one assumes that the peaks from the two complexes represent 100% of the intensity of the signal, the two peaks corresponding to the complexes with bPrP in the cases of peptides [140–160] and [145–165] represent only 36 and 42%, respectively, of the total intensity of all three peaks. This suggests that these peptides effectively compete with bPrP for binding with the antibody.

The characterization of the binding between 1E5 and the three selected peptides, two of which are assumed to be competing, was performed with a different procedure using SPR. A modified protocol (similar to the one described ear-

lier for MS) was developed and tested. Rather than the antibody, the antigen was immobilized and the antibody, after mixing with the peptides, was flowed across the surface. The binding response was followed on the sensorgram (Fig. 4E). In all cases, binding between the surface and components of the mixture of antibody with or without peptide was detected.
