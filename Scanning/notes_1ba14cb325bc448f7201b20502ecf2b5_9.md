### 3. 地球的五带

The diagram illustrates the five climate zones on Earth, defined by latitude lines and their relationship to the sun's rays:

- **北寒带 (North Polar Zone):** Located above the Arctic Circle (北极圈) at  $66.5^\circ$ . It is characterized by **有极昼极夜** (Polar day and polar night).
- **北温带 (North Temperate Zone):** Located between the Arctic Circle ( $66.5^\circ$ ) and the Tropic of Cancer (北回归线) at  $23.5^\circ$ . It is characterized by **无极昼极夜, 直射** (No polar day/night, direct rays).
- **热带 (Tropical Zone):** Located between the Tropic of Cancer ( $23.5^\circ$ ) and the Equator (赤道) at  $0^\circ$ . It is characterized by **有阳光直射** (Direct sunlight).
- **南温带 (South Temperate Zone):** Located between the Equator ( $0^\circ$ ) and the Tropic of Capricorn (南回归线) at  $23.5^\circ$ . It is characterized by **无极昼极夜, 直射** (No polar day/night, direct rays).
- **南寒带 (South Polar Zone):** Located below the Tropic of Capricorn ( $23.5^\circ$ ). It is characterized by **有极昼极夜** (Polar day and polar night).
