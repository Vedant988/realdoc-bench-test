7.7 几何形式核心笔记

(1) 复数  $z = a + bi$   $\xrightarrow{\text{一一对应}}$  点  $Z(a, b)$   
 $\xrightarrow{\text{一一对应}}$  向量  $\vec{OZ} = (a, b)$ 。

(2) “ $\cdot i$ ”的几何意义：逆时针旋转  $90^\circ$ 。

例如,  $3 + 2i \xrightarrow{\text{逆时针旋转 } 90^\circ} (3 + 2i) \cdot i = -2 + 3i$ 。

![Diagram showing the multiplication of a complex number by i. Point Z(3, 2) is in the first quadrant. Point Z'(-2, 3) is in the second quadrant. A 90-degree counter-clockwise rotation is indicated from Z to Z'.](0abb4429602314f1eeb4f001e0fe6548_7_img.webp)

$Z(3, 2) \xrightarrow{\text{逆时针旋转 } 90^\circ} Z'(-2, 3)$

(3) “ $\div i$ ”的几何意义：顺时针旋转  $90^\circ$ 。

例如,  $3 + 2i \xrightarrow{\text{顺时针旋转 } 90^\circ} \frac{3 + 2i}{i} = 2 - 3i$ 。

![Diagram showing the division of a complex number by i. Point Z(3, 2) is in the first quadrant. Point Z'(2, -3) is in the fourth quadrant. A 90-degree clockwise rotation is indicated from Z to Z'.](0abb4429602314f1eeb4f001e0fe6548_11_img.webp)

$Z(3, 2) \xrightarrow{\text{顺时针旋转 } 90^\circ} Z'(2, -3)$

(4) “ $\bar{z}$ ”的几何意义：与  $z$  关于  $x$  轴对称。
