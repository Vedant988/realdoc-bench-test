![A geometric diagram showing a rectangle ABCD. Point E is on the extension of side AD. Point F is the midpoint of side BC. A line segment EF is drawn, intersecting side DC at point G. The vertices are labeled A (bottom-left), B (bottom-right), C (top-right), and D (top-left). E is above D, F is on BC, and G is on DC.](18209462299ab36aa71814b41697eb63_6_img.webp)

解:  $\because$  四边形ABCD为平行四边形  
 $\therefore DE \parallel FC, AD = BC$   
 $\because DE : AD = 1 : 3 \therefore DE : BC = 1 : 3$   
 $\text{又} \because F \text{ 为 } BC \text{ 中点} \therefore DE : FC = 2 : 3$   
 $\text{又} \because DE \parallel FC \therefore \triangle DEG \sim \triangle CFG$

$$\therefore \frac{DE}{FC} = \frac{2}{3} \text{ 为相似比}$$

$$\therefore \frac{S_{\triangle DEG}}{S_{\triangle CFG}} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}$$
