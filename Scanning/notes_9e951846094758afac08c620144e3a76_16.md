 $S_{\text{梯形ABCD}} =$            

![Diagram of a trapezoid ABCD with parallel sides AB and CD. A diagonal AC is drawn, and a line segment BD is drawn. They intersect at point O. The area of triangle AOB is labeled 1, triangle BOC is labeled 2, triangle COD is labeled 4, and triangle AOD is labeled 2. The vertices are labeled A, B, C, D, and O.](65f0d4a43431018144a5420ed1d1e0f9_5_img.webp)

解:  $\because AB \parallel CD$

$\therefore \triangle AOB \sim \triangle COD$

即  $\frac{AO}{CO} = \frac{AB}{CD}$   $\because AB=1, CD=2, AC=6$

$\therefore \frac{AO}{CO} = \frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow CO = 2AO$

又  $\because AC = AO + CO = 3AO \Rightarrow 3AO = 6, AO = 2$

$\therefore \frac{S_{\triangle AOB}}{S_{\triangle COD}} = \left(\frac{AB}{CD}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{S_{\triangle COD}}$

$\therefore S_{\triangle COD} = 4$

$\because S_{\triangle AOB}$  与  $S_{\triangle BOC}$  (不同底, 同高)

且底之比为  $OA:OC = 1:2$

$\therefore S_{\triangle AOB} : S_{\triangle BOC} = 1:2 \quad \therefore S_{\triangle AOB} = 1$

$\therefore S_{\triangle BOC} = 2$ . 同理:  $S_{\triangle AOD}$  与  $S_{\triangle COD}$  (不同底, 同高)

且底之比为  $OA:OC = 1:2 \therefore S_{\triangle AOD} : S_{\triangle COD} = 1:2$
