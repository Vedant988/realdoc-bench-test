$$\text{海伦公式: } S_{\Delta} = \sqrt{p(p-a)(p-b)(p-c)}$$

( $p$  为  $p = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$ ,  $a, b, c$  为三角形三边长)

$$\text{那么: } S_{\Delta DQC} = \sqrt{\frac{11}{2} \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 2) \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 4) \cdot (\frac{11}{2} - 5)}$$

$$\begin{aligned} (p = \frac{2+4+5}{2} = \frac{11}{2}) \\ = \sqrt{\frac{11}{2} \cdot \frac{7}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}} \\ = \frac{\sqrt{231}}{4} \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} \therefore S_{\Delta DQC} &= \frac{1}{2} \times CQ \times DP \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times DP = DP \end{aligned}$$

$$\therefore DP = \frac{\sqrt{231}}{4} \quad \text{根据 ①}$$

$$S_{\text{四边形}ABCD} = 9 \times \text{高} = 9DP = \frac{9\sqrt{231}}{4}$$
