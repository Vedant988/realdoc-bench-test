Figure 5 consists of six panels (A-F) showing the effect of Akt overexpression (AOE) on aging-induced changes in various proteins. Each panel includes a representative gel blot at the top and a bar graph below it. The bar graphs show the ratio of phosphorylated protein to total protein (loading control,  $\alpha$ -Tubulin) for four groups: WT-Young, WT-Old, AOE-Young, and AOE-Old. Statistical significance is indicated by asterisks (\*, #).
