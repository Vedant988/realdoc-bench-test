A 3D surface plot representing a function of two variables,  $x$  and  $y$ . The  $x$ -axis ranges from 1 to 19, and the  $y$ -axis ranges from S1 to S19. The vertical axis represents the function value, ranging from -1.5 to 3. The surface is colored according to a legend that maps values to color ranges:

- 2.5-3 (Dark Blue)
- 2-2.5 (Light Blue)
- 1.5-2 (Medium Blue)
- 1-1.5 (Red)
- 0.5-1 (Dark Red)
- 0-0.5 (Light Yellow)
- -0.5-0 (Yellow)
- -1--0.5 (Dark Red)
- -1.5--1 (Light Blue)

The surface shows a complex shape with a local maximum near  $x=4, y=S13$  and a local minimum near  $x=10, y=S1$ .
