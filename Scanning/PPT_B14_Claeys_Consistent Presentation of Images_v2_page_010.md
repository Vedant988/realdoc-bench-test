![A graph titled 'Monitor Characteristic Curve' showing Luminance (cd/m²) on a logarithmic y-axis versus Digital Driving Level on a linear x-axis. The curve starts at a low luminance level for low driving levels and increases exponentially as the driving level increases. An arrow from the 'Ambient Light' label points to the initial flat part of the curve.](bd6c366fd44c1b3bf71dc43426c7f4ae_4_img.webp)

The graph illustrates the relationship between the digital driving level and the resulting luminance on a monitor. The y-axis represents Luminance in  $\text{cd/m}^2$  on a logarithmic scale, ranging from 0.01 to 100. The x-axis represents the Digital Driving Level on a linear scale, ranging from 0 to 300. The curve shows that luminance remains relatively constant at low driving levels (around 0.02  $\text{cd/m}^2$ ) and then increases exponentially as the driving level increases, reaching approximately 100  $\text{cd/m}^2$  at a driving level of 250.
